## Gated DeltaNet (equazioni esatte, verificate)
- Reference: `transformers` Qwen3_5GatedDeltaNet + fallback puro-torch `fla`
  (chunk vs recurrent err 4e-9) + `fla.naive_recurrent` (nostra recurrence err 1e-7).
- Formula: conv k=4+SiLU → split [2048|2048|6144] → reshape q,k 16 head + repeat×3
  (GVA), v 48 head → beta=sigmoid, g=-exp(A)·softplus(a+dt) → qk **L2 vera**
  (x/sqrt(sum+eps), non RMS) → scale 1/sqrt(128) → delta recurrence →
  RMSNormGated (w diretto, ones-init) × silu(z) → out_proj. Residual:
  h=x+GDN(LNin(x)); y=h+MLP(LNpa(h)). RMSNorm semplice usa (1+w), zero-init.
- C++ `GdnBlockF32` vs golden tiny-block transformers: prefill err 1.8e-7 PASS;
  token-by-token (conv+state carry) vs prefill: out 3.7e-8, state 3.7e-9 PASS.
- `attn_norm` GGUF non usato dal path lineare (mappato LNin, da confermare su full-attn).
